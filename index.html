Fix It: Home basics

🚰 Leaking tap

Place a container underneath and note where the water is coming from. If you can safely reach the isolation valve, turn it off. Contact a plumber if the leak continues.

💡 Electricity problem

If you smell burning, see sparks, or feel a shock, keep away and seek urgent professional help. Never open electrical panels or touch exposed wires.

💧 Damp wall

Take a photo, note when the dampness appears, and check for nearby plumbing leaks. Don't paint over dampness before the cause is identified.

🚪 Door won't close

Check whether an object is blocking it and look for visibly loose hinges. Don't force the door if the frame appears damaged.

When to call a professional

Electrical faults

Use a qualified electrician for wiring, exposed cables, repeated tripping, or burning smells.

Plumbing leaks

Call a plumber for persistent leaks, burst pipes, sewage, or hidden water damage.

Structural cracks

Get a qualified building professional to assess widening cracks, sagging ceilings, or movement in walls.

Safety first

If there's immediate danger, move away from the affected area and contact the appropriate emergency service.
